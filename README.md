# Sales-performance-analysis
Sales performance and profitability analysis using Power BI and DAX

Sales Performance & Profitability Analysis

Project Overview

This project explores sales performance and profitability using Power BI. It focuses on regional performance, sales channels, shipping methods, and order returns.

The dashboard is designed to help users monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), compare business performance, and identify areas that may require further investigation.

Dashboard Preview

"Sales Performance and Profitability Dashboard" (Dashboard.view.png)

Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Power BI
- DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)
- Data visualization and business intelligence

- Sales, profit, order count, and profit margin KPIs
- Regional sales performance and target comparisons
- Sales and profit analysis by channel
- Return rate analysis by region
- Order analysis by shipping method
- Interactive filters for region, channel, and shipping method
- Key business insights based on the data

Business Questions

- Which regions contribute most to sales and profit?
- How do sales and profitability vary across channels?
- Which shipping methods account for the largest share of orders?
- How do return rates differ across regions and shipping methods?
- Which performance indicators require further investigation?

Key Findings

- Online Channel Performance: The online channel accounts for 55.83% of total sales, highlighting its significant contribution to overall revenue.
- Regional Performance: The West region records the lowest sales, at approximately $302.7K, and the highest return rate, making it a key area for further investigation.
- Shipping Method Analysis: Standard shipping accounts for 63.68% of total orders, representing the most widely used shipping method.
- Profitability: The overall profit margin is 27.21%, providing an overview of the business's profitability.

These findings highlight key patterns in sales performance, regional results, shipping preferences, and profitability.
